# The Young Knight’s Quest
### A Grade 1 Visual & Mental Math Adventure for Homeschool Families

**The Young Knight’s Quest** is a medieval story-driven math adventure that guides children and parents through a complete **Grade 1 Visual & Mental Math** curriculum. Instead of mindless drill worksheets or chaotic arcade distractions, children journey alongside **Master Aldous** (a voiced royal tutor) across a 23-mission Kingdom Map—moving step-by-step from **Concrete** pictures to **Pictorial** math diagrams to **Abstract** mental math.

* **100% Free & Offline-Capable:** Runs directly in any browser (iPad, tablet, Mac, or PC) as a Progressive Web App (PWA) with zero ads, zero logins, and zero subscriptions.
* **Built for Parents & Kids Sitting Together (or Independent Play):** Includes a live, toggleable **Parent Teaching Sidebar** with Socratic scripts, visual & mental math heuristics, hands-on tabletop activities, and Classical Christian wonder prompts.
* **Zero External Runtime Dependencies:** Built entirely with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, custom hand-crafted SVG illustrations, and static `.wav` voice clips.

## How the Game Works: A Tour of the 3 Screens

### 1. The Kingdom Overworld Map
When you launch the game, your Squire stands on the **Kingdom Overworld Map**, spanning **4 Acts and 23 Missions** along the King's Highway:
* **Click any unlocked Mission pin** on the map to preview the unit topic in the bottom banner, then press **Start Level**.
* **Rank & Progression:** As your child masters missions, they advance in knightly rank (from *Woodland Page* all the way to *Dragon Vanquisher*).
* **Mission Control (Top Bar):** Need to jump ahead to match where your child is in their Grade 1 math studies? Click **Mission Control** in the top navigation bar to jump directly to any of the 23 missions, unlock all missions, or reset progress.

### 2. The Mission Workspace (`Lesson` + `Play & Practice`)
Every mission has two modes at the top right of the parchment sheet:

* **Mode A — `Lesson` (Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract):**
  * Before quizzing your child, Master Aldous walks through a visual story introducing the math concept using clear visual math models (Ten-Frames, Number Bonds, Make-10 Bridges, Bar Models, Base-10 Towers, Clocks, and Coins).
  * Includes interactive **"Try It Together"** check-ins so your child actively participates during the lesson.
* **Mode B — `Play & Practice` (Adaptive 3-Tier Mastery):**
  * Children solve dynamic story problems to earn **5 Golden Shields** per quest (and **3 Stars** per unit across 3 progressive quests):
    * **Tier 1 (Concrete):** Smaller numbers with full visual scaffolding.
    * **Tier 2 (Standard):** Full unit number range with visual math diagrams.
    * **Tier 3 (Mental Math):** Encourages picturing the strategy in the mind without counting one-by-one.
  * **Smart Adaptive Scaffolding:** A child cannot just guess their way through—wrong answers require solving the concept cleanly, and two misses in a row gently step down the difficulty tier and reveal Master Aldous's visual coaching hint.
  * **Parent Corner Shortcuts (Bottom Right):**
    * **`Hide / Show Picture`:** Toggle the visual diagram on or off to test mental math.
    * **`✓ Answered Aloud`:** If your child shouts out the right answer before tapping a tile, click this button to award the strike immediately!
    * **`↻ Next`:** Deal a fresh problem at any time.

### 3. The Live Parent Teaching Sidebar
Click the **`Parent Guide`** button in the top-right corner of any mission to slide open a split-screen teaching guide (without covering up your child's screen!). It updates live as you move through the lesson and provides:
1. **Live On-Screen Coaching:** Explains the exact visual model currently shown on your child's screen.
2. **Difficulty Tier Switcher:** Manually jump between **Tier 1 (Concrete)**, **Tier 2 (Standard)**, or **Tier 3 (Mental)** at any time.
3. **Say Together (Socratic Script):** Word-for-word questions you can ask your child to help them discover the math pattern themselves.
4. **Classical Christian Wonder & Order:** Connects mathematical order, symmetry, and faithfulness to a sense of wonder.
5. **Visual & Mental Math Strategy:** Explains the core mental math heuristic (such as *Make-10*, *Subtract-from-10*, or *Part-Whole Bar Models*) so parents understand *why* visual mastery teaches it this way.
6. **Hands-On Tabletop Activity:** Quick ideas using everyday household items (coins, LEGO bricks, snacks) to reinforce the lesson away from the screen.

### 4. Interactive Math Blocks Workshop (`Math Blocks` Button)
Click **`Math Blocks`** in the top bar from anywhere in the app to open a free-form digital manipulative mat with **7 interactive stations** covering the entire Grade 1 curriculum:
1. **Bonds to 10 (Units 1–4):** Interactive Number Bonds, Ten-Frames, Number Paths, and Cross-Out Subtraction.
2. **Make-10 Bridge (Units 5–7):** Visual Double Ten-Frames that animate sliding counters to complete a Ten.
3. **Shapes & Length (Units 8–10):** Polygon corner/side inspector, Ordinal Number lineups (1st–10th), and non-standard block measurement.
4. **Bar Models (Units 11, 13, 17):** Part-Whole and Comparison ("How Many More/Fewer?") Bar Models.
5. **Tens & Regroup (Units 12–17):** Base-10 Towers and Loose Ones up to 100.
6. **Groups & Halves (Units 14–15):** Equal groups (multiplication readiness without premature `×` symbols) and Halves/Fourths fraction shields.
7. **Clock & Coins (Units 18–19):** Interactive analog/digital Watchtower Clock (hour & half-hour) and Royal Coin Purse (pennies, nickels, dimes, quarters).
* Each station includes both an **`Explore & Teach`** sandbox mode and a **`Practice & Quiz Me`** mode!

## Complete Grade 1 Curriculum Scope (Units 1–19)
The campaign spans **23 Action Missions across 4 Acts**, covering **19 core Grade 1 Visual & Mental Math units** plus **4 Act Boss Battles**:

### Act IV — The Dragon’s Mountain to 100 (Units 16–19)
19. **Light the Signal Beacons** — *Unit 16: Numbers to 100 (Tens & Ones to 100)*
20. **Storm the Outer Ramparts** — *Unit 17: Addition & Subtraction Within 100 (Place Value & Bar Models)*

23. **FINAL GRADE 1 BOSS: The Red Dragon** — *Complete Grade 1 (Units 1–19) Mastery Crown*

## Architecture, Privacy & Quality Assurance (For Portfolio Viewers)

* **100% Client-Side Privacy (Zero Data Collection):** All progress is saved locally in the family's browser via `localStorage` (`young_knights_quest_v2`). No personal data, analytics, or telemetry ever leaves the user's device.
* **Custom Vector Art Engine ([`visuals.js`](./visuals.js)):** Zero system emojis or third-party image dependencies. Every castle, medieval banner, ten-frame, number bond, bar model, analog clock, and coin is dynamically rendered as crisp, scalable SVG artwork.
* **Pre-Rendered Static Neural Voice Bank ([`audio/`](./audio/)):** Master Aldous's 57 coaching, praise, and boss battle lines are pre-rendered into static `.wav` files with a Web Audio API chime fallback, requiring zero cloud TTS API keys or runtime inference.
* **Automated Verification Suite ([`tests/verify_all.py`](./tests/verify_all.py)):** Includes a 7-stage automated QA harness that fuzz-tests 2,000+ generated problems across all 23 missions and 3 tiers, verifying that story nouns, numbers, and rendered SVG elements match 100% of the time. Run it locally with:
  ```bash
  python3 tests/verify_all.py
  ```
* **Original Content Notice:** All stories, characters, dialogue, SVG artwork, and generated math exercises in this project are original works created independently for homeschool and educational use, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any third-party curriculum publisher.
