# Anti-AI-Slop & Artisanal Design Craft

This project serves families and young children in a Classical Christian homeschool setting. Every screen must feel like a handcrafted heirloom storybook, brass-and-wood workshop, or living 3D garden—**never** like a generic AI-generated SaaS dashboard or a re-colored clone of another page.

## 1. Banned AI-Slop Anti-Patterns
1. **No "Pill-for-Everything" Syndrome:**
   - Do **not** wrap every unit label, tag, progress readout, status, or subtitle inside `border-radius: 999px` pastel capsules.
   - Reserve rounded badges strictly for true interactive toggles or a single focal crest; render metadata with clean typographic hierarchy (small-caps, hairline rules, engraved plaques, ribbons, or understated serif italics).
2. **No Grade-to-Grade Template Cloning:**
   - Never copy Grade 1's map/layout and swap colors to make Kindergarten or Grade 2.
   - Top header utilities (`← All Grades`, progress, workshop, sound) and bottom action bar may share ergonomic consistency, but each grade's **world canvas** must have its own spatial metaphor and visual soul (e.g., Grade 1: Illuminated Cartographic Kingdom Map; Kindergarten: Living 3D Four Seasons Garden Walk).
3. **No Generic SaaS Card-Grid Boilerplate:**
   - Avoid sterile white cards with identical 1px borders, thin gray horizontal dividers, stacked bullet lists, and generic gradient CTA buttons. Use tactile framing: illuminated manuscript borders, carved timber plaques, linen/vellum grain, wax seals, or atmospheric depth.
4. **No Flat Primitive Placeholders:**
   - Never draw a "tree" as two overlapping circles, a "brook" as a flat blue stripe, or a "world" as four vertical gradient bands with dashed lines.
   - Every visual scene must have real **depth planes** (sky/atmosphere, distant ridges/canopy, midground architecture/flora, foreground framing), **directional lighting & cast shadows**, **layered organic silhouettes**, and **ambient life** (drifting leaves, falling snow, shimmering water, fireflies, sunbeams—always honoring `prefers-reduced-motion`).
5. **No Neon/Purple-Cyan AI Gradients or Emoji Clutter:**
   - Zero system emojis in UI/SVG/code; zero trendy SaaS glassmorphism or glowing neon accents. Use warm, natural Classical pigments (deep forest, parchment cream, burnished brass, terracotta, slate blue, warm oak).

## 2. Age-Appropriate Interaction & Guided Movement
* **Kindergarteners Do Not Steer 3D Cameras:** Young children (ages 5–6) must never be forced to wrestle with WASD keys or virtual thumbsticks to find a lesson.
* **Parent-Triggered, Self-Moving World:** When a parent (or child) clicks a walk, season, or milestone, the **character and camera glide smoothly through the 3D/living world by themselves** along the scenic path, arriving naturally at the next lantern, cottage, or garden arbor.

## 3. Mandatory Craft Checklist Before Finishing Any Visual Task
1. **Spatial Distinctiveness:** Does this grade/page have an unmistakable, original world identity?
2. **Zero Pill Spam:** Count capsule pills (`border-radius: 999px`) on screen—eliminate decorative pill clutter in favor of real typography, ribbons, or carved plaques.
3. **Atmospheric Depth & Materiality:** Are there clear lighting, shadow, texture, and foreground/midground/background layers?
4. **Live Visual Inspection:** Always launch the page in a real browser (e.g. via local HTTP server + visual inspection) and inspect rendered screenshots across states before declaring work complete. Never trust headless DOM tests alone for visual quality.
